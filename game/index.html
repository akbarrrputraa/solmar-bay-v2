HOW TO PLAY

WASD / arrows — walk, or drive when in a car  ·  Shift sprint
E — steal / enter the nearest car, or get out  ·  Space handbrake
Mouse — aim  ·  Click — shoot (on foot)  ·  Q — switch weapon
R — change radio station  ·  M — mute  ·  G — garage (near the garage)
P / Esc — pause, save, resume  ·  Enter — start next mission

Follow the yellow marker on the minimap. Crimes raise your wanted level (stars, top right): 1–2 stars bring patrol cars, 3 adds roadblocks, 4–5 bring the police helicopter. Break line of sight to cool down. If you are busted or wasted you wake up at the clinic and lose some cash. Finish all 8 missions to pull off the Big Score.
